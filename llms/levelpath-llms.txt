# Levelpath

> Levelpath unifies intake, contracts, supplier management, and strategic sourcing with native AI. It is a procurement platform designed to cut cycle times, secure savings, and provide visibility for enterprise teams.

## Core Product
- Levelpath Platform: https://www.levelpath.com/platform - Overview of the unified procurement platform and AI capabilities.
- AI Agents: https://www.levelpath.com/agents - Specialized AI agents for contract discovery, negotiations, and sourcing.
- Front Door to Procure: https://www.levelpath.com/products/front-door-to-procure - Simplified intake process for employees to request purchases.
- Strategic Sourcing: https://www.levelpath.com/products/sourcing - Tools for RFP management, supplier selection, and negotiation.
- Contracts: https://www.levelpath.com/products/contracts - Repository and lifecycle management for supplier agreements.
- Risk Management: https://www.levelpath.com/products/risk - Third-party risk management (TPRM) and supplier due diligence.
- Invoice Automation: https://www.levelpath.com/products/invoice-automation - Automated invoice processing and payment workflows.
- Pipeline: https://www.levelpath.com/products/pipeline - Project tracking, reporting, and procurement analytics.
